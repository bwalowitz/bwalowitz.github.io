PRODUCT SPEC · DRAFT 1 · APRIL 2026
FREIGHT PILOT
AI prospect agent for logistics salespeople
The Problem

A logistics salesperson spends the first 2–3 hours of their day doing things that aren't sales: searching ZoomInfo, verifying contacts, researching companies, writing outreach. By the time they make a call, they've already spent their sharpest hours on admin. Freight Pilot gives that time back.

Target Customer
Small-to-mid freight brokerages 2–15 salespeople Commission-based No enterprise tooling

Individual salespeople who pay out of pocket for an edge. Not the IT department. Not the VP of Sales. The rep on quota who wants more dials with less prep.

What the Agent Does
1
You describe what you need
Natural language. “Give me 10 food & beverage manufacturers in the southeast, looking for a transportation manager or logistics director, who might be shopping for a new TL provider.”
2
Agent searches and enriches
Pulls company data, verifies the right contact title, finds email + phone, checks LinkedIn activity, scans for intent signals like job postings, news, capacity complaints, and new warehouse openings.
3
Agent researches the person
Recent LinkedIn posts, shared interests, tenure, anything that creates a real conversation hook, not just a first name in a template.
4
Agent writes the opener
One personalized first-contact email or LinkedIn message based on the intel gathered. Not a template, specific to this person, this company, this moment.
5
You receive a Prospect Card
Everything above, formatted cleanly. Name, title, verified contact info, intel brief, suggested opener. Ready to call or send.
What It Is Not

Not a directory. Not a CRM. Not another ZoomInfo clone. Those tools hand you a list, you still do all the work. Freight Pilot does the work and hands you a person who's ready to contact, with context, a reason to call, and something to say.

Business Model
Model Pay-per-prospect (credits)
Starting price $5 / prospect card
Volume tiers $4 at 50+/mo · $3 at 200+/mo
Entry point Free trial: 5 cards, no credit card
Future Team subscriptions for small brokerages
If one converted prospect earns a rep $2,000–$10,000 in commission, a $5 card that helped land it is a 400x–2000x return. The price is never the objection, quality and trust are.
Why This vs. ZoomInfo / Seamless AI
They sell data. We sell a ready-to-contact prospect. The research, the intel, and the opener are included.
They need training. You talk to this like a person. No filters, no Boolean, no learning curve.
They're expensive. $15k–$20k/yr for enterprise seats. We're $5 per card, no commitment.
They go stale. Agent checks signals at the moment you ask, not from a database last updated 6 months ago.

MVP — What Gets Built First

A single-purpose web app. User describes the type of prospect they want. Agent returns 1–10 prospect cards. User can purchase more credits. That's it. No dashboard, no CRM sync, no team features, until it's proven.
