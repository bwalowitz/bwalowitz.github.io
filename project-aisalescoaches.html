AI Sales and Onboarding Coaches

Built for logistics teams that hire new SDRs. Teaches the language, helps answer tough questions, and lets reps practice calls with clear feedback.

What this is

Training that feels like a coach, not a script

I built a set of AI coaches for sales teams:

  • Jargon Coach — explains logistics terms in plain language and tests understanding.
  • Objection Coach — it suggests simple ways to respond, explains why they work, and offers a couple of alternatives.
  • Cold Call Coach — runs short mock calls and gives direct feedback on pacing, clarity, and next steps.

I handled strategy, design, prompts, content, and testing with client subject matter experts.

Objectives

What we set out to do

  • New reps get up to speed faster.
  • Make sure everyone explains our services the same simple way.
  • Give managers a simple way to coach without more meetings.
  • Make practice easy: short sessions, quick feedback, try again.
How it works

Short sessions, clear feedback, saved wins

  • Pick a coach and a topic (term, objection, or call).
  • Do a 3–5 minute session.
  • Get plain feedback and one or two clearer ways to say it.
  • Save notes to a simple library you can search later.

Privacy and consent are built in. No personal data in practice sets.

Results

What improved

  • New reps get up to speed faster.
  • Clear, plain language that sticks.
  • Fewer retakes in practice before reps feel ready to call.
  • Managers spend less time fixing slides and more time on strategy.
