AI Usage Principles

How Not to Misuse AI
A practical guide for people using AI in professional or creative work

⚠️ Principle 1: Know Enough to Know When It’s Wrong

If you can’t tell whether the AI is right or wrong, don’t use it for that task.

AI sounds confident even when it’s confidently wrong.
Misuse happens when people ask AI for answers in domains they don’t understand.

🛠️ Principle 2: Experiment Freely — Deploy Carefully

Tinker all you want. Break things. Learn. But when you're building for a team or an organization, what you build might become a foundation.

If someone else will depend on your output, you’re responsible for the structure.

🧠 Principle 3: AI Is Not a Brain — You Are

AI is a tool. You are the thinker. Don’t outsource decisions to autocomplete.

There’s no such thing as “AI made me do it.”

🧭 Principle 4: Clarity In → Clarity Out

AI mirrors your direction. The clearer your ask, the better your result.

Vague prompt = vague output.
Specific context = smarter responses.

⏳ Principle 5: Don’t Skip the Thinking

AI is a speed boost, not a shortcut to wisdom.

It can’t do your core job — it can only help you do it faster, better, or differently.

🤝 Principle 6: Collaborate With It — Don’t Delegate Blindly

AI is a creative partner, not an employee. You don’t assign it work — you work with it.

If you don’t know what needs to be done, AI won’t magically figure it out.

Final Word

This isn’t about rules. It’s about stewardship.
We’re the ones shaping how AI shows up in the world. Let’s do it with clarity, curiosity, and responsibility.
