Work

Redwood Logistics

Brand identity and internal agency system. Six companies branded into one clear Redwood story so teams could work faster and stay consistent.

Overview

Six into one

I led the rebrand that brought six companies into one Redwood brand. The goal was simple. One name, one look, one voice. The work had to be usable by marketing, sales, HR, and ops without slowing anyone down.

We set objectives up front. Unify the identity. Make decks and one pagers faster to build. Give sales a single story and a clean call to action. Reduce one off requests by giving people good defaults.

System

Clear rules that are easy to follow

I audited live materials across the six companies, kept what worked, and removed what didn’t. Then I codified the core pieces. Logo usage and clear space. Color palette anchored by Redwood Red plus tight neutrals. Readable type scales for headlines and body. A light graphic language that nods to network movement without fighting the message.

Everything lives in a shared library so updates roll out everywhere. New people can plug in on day one and ship without a long ramp.

Templates

Templates that speed real work

I built templates for the tools teams actually use. PowerPoint for sales with cover, proof, solution, and next step patterns. InDesign for print with a consistent grid and type rhythm. Social kits with safe crops and text overlays. Case study and one pager patterns that lead with problem, move, and outcome so the value is obvious.

The result is less time reinventing slides and more time talking to customers.

Application

One look across every touchpoint

We applied the system to brochures, banners, signage, invites, and event tables so the brand feels the same in print, on screen, and in person. Environmental mockups stress tested contrast and legibility on rough materials. Texture is used as a support, not the star.

Outcome. Fewer one offs, faster handoffs, and a stronger signal in every channel.
